Undergraduate research · CSUF · 2026–present

GroundTruth

A computer-vision classifier that flags visible slope-instability indicators (cracks, scarps, loose debris) in ground-level photos. It runs a frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 backbone with test-time augmentation and a calibrated logistic-regression probe. A SegFormer input gate strips out sky and water and refuses photos the model can't assess. The 200-image dataset is labelled by site, so evaluation measures generalization rather than memorization.

  • 85.1%accuracy, site-grouped 5-fold CV
  • 0.944ROC AUC
  • 92.9%of unstable slopes flagged at 81.2% precision

  • PyTorch
  • Hugging Face
